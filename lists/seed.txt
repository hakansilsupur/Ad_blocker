# Curated seed blocklist
#
# Hand-picked ad and tracking endpoints, kept small enough to read and audit.
# It ships with the repo so the blocker works before (and without) any
# download; `python -m adblock update` merges the large public lists on top.
#
# Blocking a domain also blocks its subdomains, so `doubleclick.net` covers
# `stats.g.doubleclick.net` and friends.
#
# Anything here that breaks a site or app for you goes in lists/allowlist.txt,
# which always wins and survives updates.
